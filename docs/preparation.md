Guild preparation matters as much as individual execution.

“One fight in GvG is often decided by 100 upgrades in the Guild talents.” — Bai Wutong

Prioritize the shared upgrades before entering the battlefield.

Well, whenever you enter the Battlefield for GvG, you want to make sure your Guild (not just you) is as prepared as possible.

Work through the following layers in order.

### Layer 1 EX-Skills

As mentioned above, the power of your martial arts depends mainly on your EX-Skill, so the first thing you will want to do is unlock it.

To do so head to your guild menu and select Techniques.

In the Martial technique category, scroll down, till you find the EX-Skill for your corresponding martial art and level it up.

### Layer 2 GvG Stat-boosts

In the same Technique Tab, at the top you will find the following Teachings.

![Preparation and guild techniques — source illustration 45](/images/gvg/image46.png)

Leveling these up will provide you with additional stat boosts for GvG.

Your first priority is getting up the 3 general stats.

This is mainly to bolster your survivability, so you won’t collapse on first contact with the enemy.

![Preparation and guild techniques — source illustration 46](/images/gvg/image47.png)

Next you will want to level your path specific boosts.

![Preparation and guild techniques — source illustration 47](/images/gvg/image48.png)

Followed by the conditional GvG buffs.

These will provide you situational buffs like bonus damage to Bulwarks at a certain range or a damage boost when close to a tree carrier.

![Preparation and guild techniques — source illustration 48](/images/gvg/image49.png)

![Preparation and guild techniques — source illustration 49](/images/gvg/image50.png)

These buffs are shared across the guild.

The more points guild members invest, the more everyone benefits.

Underleveled guild stats leave the whole team at a disadvantage.

Make GvG techniques a shared preparation priority.

The guide recommends requiring members to invest in these shared techniques.

Every guild member should prioritize these upgrades.

You are not at a disadvantage if you clear Skybond later in a season.

You should be able to clear breaking army even without the techniques (granted they make it easier but are not mandatory).

The start of the season is when your placements in GvG happen, it’s the most critical time, so get these up first and above all else.

The guide recommends maxing these upgrades before spending points elsewhere.

Guild leaders, you can and SHOULD demand your members to level these.

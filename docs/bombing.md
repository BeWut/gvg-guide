---
title: "Coordinated bombing"
description: "Chain protection, control, antiheal, and damage into a coordinated engage."
---

A bomb chains protection, crowd control, antiheal, and burst damage to eliminate a group before its healers can recover. Adapt the sequence to your roster; the source particularly recommends a Mo Blade and Nameless Sword.

## Engage sequence

1. Coordinate **Flute of the Tides** casts.
2. The tank prepares their shield.
3. Nameless Sword grants the tank Qi protection with its EX skill, reducing the risk of a Qi break and execution.
4. The tank closes the gap. **Cloud Steps** can reach a group of backliners; aim for the healer when possible, while prioritizing a pull that catches several enemies.
5. Depending on the tank’s secondary weapon, use the **Nameless Spear** or **Stormbreaker Spear** EX skill.
6. If available, a commander applies **antiheal** to the enemy.
7. The tank uses the **Mo Blade EX skill** to pull and control the group.

## Follow up by role

- **Ranged DPS:** Focus fire on the grouped enemies and commit your damage. Use your EX skill here when appropriate for your build, such as Dust builds.
- **Skirmishers:** Reach and kill the healer quickly. Twinblades and gauntlets can also engage other targets because their EX skills shut down healing.
- **Healers:** Keep your squad alive. Add damage with Lion’s Roar, Leaping Toad, or Flaming Meteor when healing allows.
- **Inkwell Fan:** Trap the grouped enemies with your EX skill.
- **Additional tanks:** Chain another pull shortly before the first expires to extend the control window.

Finish the remaining enemies together.

## Simpler alternative

The source also describes a coordinated **Flaming Meteor** engage as an effective, simpler alternative on the patch it was written for. Treat that as patch-dependent advice.

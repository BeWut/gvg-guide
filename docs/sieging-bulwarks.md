So, we have established why Bulwarks are important and that we want to get rid of at least 2 of the enemy’s Towers as soon as possible but how do we do that efficiently and without having to deal with troublesome Damage-Reduction?

Let’s first talk about where each of you should be positioned:

![Sieging bulwarks — source illustration 50](/images/gvg/image51.png)

How can ranged DPS check that they are standing at the right distance?

Use these two checks:

- If you enabled the Boss distance meter, you will be able to check manually

- If you leveled your Talents, you would see this buff on your bar.
  If it’s on 3 stacks, you are at the sweet spot.

Attacking team ranged DPS:

Try splitting into smaller groups, preferably to the sides of the lane, this way it’s harder for the enemies to harass multiple of you at the same time.

Also keep your distance from the enemy Bulwark to not enhance its Damage Reduction.

![Sieging bulwarks — source illustration 51](/images/gvg/image52.png)

![Sieging bulwarks — source illustration 52](/images/gvg/image53.png)

Attacking team Tank and melee DPS:

You may venture further forward than displayed here, especially if it’s your job to hunt down enemy healers.

Usually though, you will want to stick close to the bulwark to damage it, while remaining on your side of it, so your healer won’t need to put themselves in harms way as much to keep you alive.

Attacking team Healer:

Your position will be wherever you are needed the most.

Often this means you will find yourself as sort of a middle line between your ranged DPS and frontline.

Sadly, I see two mistakes way too often, so please avoid these in your positioning, if you can:

- Do not stand on the OPPOSITE side of the enemy tower. You have no business being there, especially not on your own.

- Unless you just slammed in with Flaming Meteor or dropped a healing field, you should stay away as to not grant it additional damage reduction. Your damage usually won’t outweigh the loss from everyone else.

![Sieging bulwarks — source illustration 53](/images/gvg/image54.png)

As the attacking team your target is not the players but the bulwark, only switch to players if you absolutely have to or if they used the “City protection” commander buff.
You will recognize if city protection has been used, when the bulwark suddenly stops taking damage for a few seconds. This is your time to wipe their defense if you can.

Bombing is highly recommended in those time windows, if you need to focus on enemy players, wipe them as fast as possible.

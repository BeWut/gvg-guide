At its very core GvG functions like a MOBA and the map is set up like most traditional games of said genre.

![Map and objectives — source illustration 2](/images/gvg/image3.jpeg)

In order to win our Guild needs to destroy the enemy team’s towers, fight and kill their Goose and then claim the other guilds tree by carrying it back to our base.

Sounds simple enough but how do we get there?
Well let’s first take a look at the Map itself and all the structures and obstacles in it.

## The Map

### Jungle

“Jungle” refers to the area between the “Lanes” so between the main ways connecting both teams bases.

Usually, each team holds control over 2 quadrants of it and tries to steal control from the other guild.

Periodically (every 5 minutes) Mobs will spawn within the Jungle (2 mobs per quadrant), these can be killed to earn Fun Coins (720 per mob) for your team.

To make sure the other team won’t steal the mob you are fighting, try to stick close to it, so you can use the execute-skill once is Qi breaks to guarantee the last hit.

![Map and objectives — source illustration 3](/images/gvg/image4.jpeg)

### Tower/Bulwark

Each team starts with 3 Bulwarks (one in each lane) and your top priority early on is taking down the other team’s towers.

Towers are tanky and deal AoE Damage around themselves.

As long as they are up, they grant your team the following benefits:

- Safety and control over the area

- Fun Coin-Generation, as long as Bulwarks aren’t destroyed they will grant your team Fun Coins over time 200 per Bulwark every 30s)

![Map and objectives — source illustration 4](/images/gvg/image5.jpeg)

![Map and objectives — source illustration 5](/images/gvg/image6.png)

Players can join Bulwarks, which will make them jump on top of it.

This grants access to the following skills:

- A pull effect similar to the Mo-Blade Ex-Skill

- The ability to toss an Axe at enemies for massive Damage

- You can also just jump off again

At the start of each GvG players can be assigned to start on top of a Bulwark already.

When sieging or defending, keep in mind that Bulwarks gain 2 kinds of damage reduction:

- Melee-Damage-Reduction, this one increases for each player within 10m around the Bulwark (both enemies and allies), up to 15 times (in the source 5% reduction per nearby player)

- Range-Damage-Reduction, if you stand further than 15m away from the tower, your damage will be reduced by 70% and by 90% if you are than 25m away.

![Map and objectives — source illustration 6](/images/gvg/image7.jpeg)

(This picture serves purely as a visualization and is not distance accurate)

Breaking a bulwark also reduces the enemy Gooses attributes.

### Goose

The Goose serves as a Guardian and can theoretically be attacked from second 1 but it is advised to break 1-2 Bulwarks first.

Similar to Bulwarks, the Goose gains 2 kinds of damage reduction:

- Melee-Damage-Reduction, increases for each player within 10m around the Goose (again both enemies and allies), up to 30 times (in the source 1,5% reduction per nearby player)

- Range-Damage-Reduction, if you stand further than 15m away from the tower, your damage will be reduced by 70% and by 90% if you are than 25m away.

### Tree

Once the Goose has been taken down the tree gets accessible and with it the real objective:

Carrying the other team’s tree over to your own Base.

One player (typically a tank) will pick up the tree and begin walking towards their own base.

The enemy Guild needs to stop this from happening by killing the tree carrier.

Meanwhile their own guild needs to defend them.

Note: Even if the tree carrier dies, the tree cannot be moved back by its own guild.

![Map and objectives — source illustration 10](/images/gvg/image11.jpeg)

The team who moves the tree the furthest towards their base wins.
Carrying it through your bases gate is an instant victory.

### Choke Points

Choke points are something everyone is familiar with; in this case they aren’t hot though.

I marked the most relevant ones on this map (every narrow pathway can turn into one, but these are the most relevant ones, since in 9/10 games both teams will pass through them while carrying the tree).

Usually this is the best place to intercept or stop the enemy team’s advances.

If you are trying to move the tree to your base, make sure to clean these out beforehand, to avoid potential disaster

![Map and objectives — source illustration 11](/images/gvg/image12.jpeg)

### Bosses

Throughout the map there will be two bosses spawning:

- First on either minute 26 or 24

- Second on either minute 16 or 14

The position is randomly chosen between the two marked locations.

This and the varying times is to prevent spawn camping.

Killing the boss grants rewards to the team landing the killing blow, no matter who did the most damage

![Map and objectives — source illustration 12](/images/gvg/image13.jpeg)

![Map and objectives — source illustration 13](/images/gvg/image14.png)

Boss rewards:

- The first boss grants a huge Fun Coin reward (in the source 6000)

- The second boss also grants Fun Coins, as well as the Blood Nirvana Buff for 5 minutes.

### Strongholds/Outposts

On each side of the map there is a Stronghold, which can be conquered by either team.

Should you decide to do so this will grant you the ability to revive at said stronghold instead of your own base, in case you die.

This can be useful if you are on the attacking team.

![Map and objectives — source illustration 14](/images/gvg/image15.jpeg)

![Map and objectives — source illustration 15](/images/gvg/image16.jpeg)

Something to keep in mind is that the enemy team can also steal your stronghold later.

Also in the source the boss will create a Dome over its arena, allowing players to enter but not to leave until its defeated.

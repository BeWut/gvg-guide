Defense uses a different approach from attacking: protect the structure and disrupt the enemy’s damage.

Before we dive deeper into the tricky subject, let’s first cover positioning once more.
In the defense your goal is to stick close enough to your Bulwark/Goose, to grant it as much damage reduction as possible (remember, your own players count), while simultaneously not clumping to much.
Else the enemy will have an easy time bombing you down.

Defense team ranged DPS:

Try to stick to the sides of the lane, so you won’t get caught in the damage thrown at your tower.

Make sure to focus fire, in an ideal cage, take out the enemy healer.

Defense team Tank and melee DPS:

While you should try to stick closer to your tower for the damage reduction, your main job is to harass and annoy the enemy.

Killing the healer is very valuable but simply pushing them back and making it hard for their backline to keep their rotations up is great already.

Defense team Healer:

Not a lot changes, you will weave back and forth, keeping your teammates alive.

That said, you are the prime candidate, to jump onto your own bulwark and snipe enemies with the hatchet toss or if they are grouped around your tower, to use the pull on it.

![Defending bulwarks — source illustration 56](/images/gvg/image57.png)

Staying close to the structure and fighting off enemies is only part of the job.

Pay attention to what a wipe lets the enemy do next.

The guide recommends avoiding a full wipe of the enemy attacking squad unless you need to take a boss. A simultaneous respawn can let them regroup with their defense and overwhelm your attacking squads.

After that they can either attack you with the help of their defense team and overwhelm you with numbers or simply proceed as before.

Your best bet is to force them into 1v1s or small skirmishes, kill their healer so they can’t siege, waste their time, kill them one by one but not all together.

If you DO end up wiping them, make sure to rush to your offence, to help them resist the inevitable onslaught.

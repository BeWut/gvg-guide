### Disclaimer

These are common GvG weapon choices and their uses. They are recommendations, not a reason to exclude players or force guildmates into weapons they dislike. Players should be able to participate with a build they enjoy.

The main factor deciding how useful a martial arts path is, is their EX-skill.
Which is a special, GvG specific skill a player can unlock through GvG talents (more about that later).

### Thundercry Blade

![Martial arts and EX skills — source illustration 16](/images/gvg/image17.png)

Why is this skill so good?

This ability is as simple as its strong. Simply because it’s the most powerful source of CC we have in this mode.

When/How do we use it?

Most commonly this skill is used to initiate a bomb on the enemy team.

One tank will try to get close to the enemy lines and begin pulling them in, if possible, the next tank will chain their EX-skill shortly before the first ends.

This is to keep the enemy immobilized while your remaining team members can freely unload their AoE damage on the enemy.

### Panacea Fan

![Martial arts and EX skills — source illustration 17](/images/gvg/image18.png)

Why is this skill so good?

Well, my little mushrooms being alive is good and this skill is the best for keeping you that way. You can imagine it as a way bigger and stronger cloudburst heal.

When/How do we use it?

Early on you can use this to keep your team’s HP topped up while taking towers.

Generally, and by far the most important use of it is to keep your teammates alive when the enemy guild tries to bomb you.

So, try to pop it when the bomb starts but before your teammates die.

### Nameless Sword

![Martial arts and EX skills — source illustration 18](/images/gvg/image19.png)

Why is this skill so good?

So far this is the only reliable teamwide Qi-Protection that we have.

When/How do we use it?

You know what’s good?

Your tank pulling enemies in so you can kill them with your sword-waves.

You know what’s not good?

Them getting Qi-broken and executed before or while they do it.

So use this whenever your tank signals/postures to go in for a bomb or to protect your Qi-bars when the enemy team tries to go for a bomb.

### Inkwell Fan

![Martial arts and EX skills — source illustration 19](/images/gvg/image20.png)

Why is this skill so good?

While its not directly CC, it is an incredibly versatile area control ability.

When/How do we use it?

There are 3 main uses for this:

- To hold multiple enemies in a tight area for easier targeting or bombing. (for example, locking them in with your skirmishers)

- To hinder the enemy tree carrier, as without a specific buff, he won’t be able to walk out of/through it.

- To protect your own tree carrier by making it difficult for enemies to reach and block him. (your allies can walk through the walls)

### Skygrasp Ropedart

![Martial arts and EX skills — source illustration 20](/images/gvg/image21.png)

Why is this skill so good?

This is mainly a setup skill, providing some control and the biggest possible damage amplification.

When/How do we use it?

Generally, whenever you try to wipe a group of enemies.

Imagine the hardest hitting attack your team can deliver and then double the damage and make it multitarget.

Combining this setup with the Goose’s red-circle attack can create a strong opportunity to wipe the enemy group.

### Everspring Umbrella

![Martial arts and EX skills — source illustration 21](/images/gvg/image22.png)

Why is this Martial Art so good?

Unlike most others on this list, this Martial art is mainly here due to how consistently it improves everyone else.

When/How do we use it?

Reduce resistance and grant your team more penetration on their damage to take towers, bosses and goose faster.

When trying to wipe the enemy team the AoE healing reduction of the EX-skill can also be a huge help.

### Soulshade Umbrella

![Martial arts and EX skills — source illustration 22](/images/gvg/image23.png)

Why is this Martial Art so good?

Nameless sword is Meta in GvG and this skill makes it easy for them to just spam their attacks.

When/How do we use it?

Very straight forward, whenever you siege or initiate a fight, drop this on your ranged DPS and switch to your main weapon.

### Skirmishers (Strategic Sword/Hengdao/Twinblades/Gauntlets)

![Martial arts and EX skills — source illustration 23](/images/gvg/image24.png)

![Martial arts and EX skills — source illustration 24](/images/gvg/image25.png)

Why did I group these?

They all serve a similar purpose but deliver on it in slightly different ways.

All of them can provide damage to towers but excel at picking out enemies.

Why are these useful?

They either provide a lot of burst damage or antihealing capabilities, making them excelent for getting rid of your enemy’s most precious players: their healers.

When should you use their special skills?

You can use (some of) them for damage on towers of course.

Mainly though you will activate them and go for the enemy backline.

Twinblades and gauntlets antiheal are the most reliable way to shut down individuals to make sure no burst heals negate your teams engage.

Two settings are particularly useful for GvG: target distance and healer target selection.

### Target Distance

The first setting should be used by everyone in this mode and can also be useful for Ranged DPS in PvE.

![Settings — source illustration 40](/images/gvg/image41.jpeg)

What this will do, is always show you the distance to your target, below their level and this works on structures in GvG as well.

![Settings — source illustration 41](/images/gvg/image42.png)

### Healer Target Selection

Heals aimed at the center of the screen can select a player who needs them less while a tank or another teammate is in danger.

Choose a targeting setting that directs healing toward the players who need it.

![Settings — source illustration 42](/images/gvg/image43.jpeg)

For healers, set target selection to **Lowest HP** rather than the center of your screen.

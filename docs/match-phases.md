---

### Phase 1 Preparation

Right when the match starts there’s a 5 minute preparation phase during which the leaders and commanders will usually form their respective teams and assign players to start out on Bulwarks.

Participants can also equip a Panacea fan and the “Royal Remedy” inner way to already fill up their vitality.

About 30 seconds before start, players should apply their food and script, as well as bathing pill buffs.

### Phase 2 Positioning

Once the actual match starts everyone heads out to their assigned positions.

Often this means the defense team heads into their own jungle and clears the mobs before heading to their respective tower to defend it.

Meanwhile the offense team will often invade the enemy’s jungle before heading to their target tower to siege it.

### Phase 3 Sieging

Next come sieging, both teams will try to take at least 1-2 towers, to cut off the enemies Fun Coin generation and clear out a way for the tree later.

Usually after the middle and one of the side towers have fallen, we transition to the next phase.

### Phase 4 Taking the Goose

This is very simple and straight forward: kill the enemy Goose and don’t get hit by it.

Around this time one team will usually get a big boost from the halftime show.

### Phase 5 Wrapping up

Once the Goose is down, simply carry the enemy guilds tree over to your base and compliment them on a good match.

Now that you have heard of the horrors, the enemy team may attempt to inflict on you, how do we actually protect ourselves?

Well, there are a few things to do:

- Since you are all smart little fox pups (else you wouldn’t be reading this guide), you probably already figured out the first step: Watch yours and the enemies positioning and be prepared to spread out.
  Keep an eye on the enemy tank, if you warn your teammates via voice once they go in. (Fox tip: if suddenly the whole enemy team starts using flutes all at once, there’s a high chance they will go in).
  Make is hard to for them to hit multiple of you.

- Healers, keep your cooldowns, especially your EX-Skill (do not hold onto it if your team is dying of course but if it’s not needed, don’t use it, safe it for this occasion).
  Wait for the first wave of damage and then pop your heal to top your team up again. Be on high alert, so you won’t get jumped in the chaos.

- Generally, get away and avoid damage as good as possible. Retreat momentarily if you need to, if they take a tower that’s unfortunate, if they take your whole team and then the tower, that’s a disaster.

- Use defensive skills, Qi-Shields, Golden Body, Shielding, you name it, pop nameless spear EX-Skill to mitigate the damage.

If you manage to survive in a decently healthy manner, this is your chance to strike back while the enemy team’s main skills are on cooldown.

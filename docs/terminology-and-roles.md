This page explains the calls, resources, and leadership roles used throughout the guide.

## Roles

### Shot caller

Refers to people who handle the general decision making during the battle and give orders to the teams as to who goes where or what needs to be done next, as well as calling for buffs from the commanders.

### Commander

Commanders in GvG fill an assistant role to the Guild leader (who will be commander by default). They can use “Fun Coins” to activate buffs or debuffs for their own guild or onto the enemy guild.
Often times Commanders will also serve as shot callers but don’t need to.

### Halftime Performer

The Halftime performer is the poor individual who will have to represent their guild in a 1v1 battle at the halftime mark of the GvG (which in the source is after 10 minutes).
Winning grants a significan’t boost to their respective guild, depending on the state of the game.

## Terminology

### Antiheal

As the name suggests, this is an effect, which can be applied in multiple ways (most commonly through a commander’s debuff) that prevents players (hopefully our enemies) from regaining hit points.

### Wipe

A term I assume most of you are familiar with.
It describes the act of eliminating all enemy players within a certain group/squad/area (for example killing all enemies who were on their attacking team).
These are huge swings in momentum and ideally happen every 3-4 minutes.

### Bomb/Bombing

Wipes are great but we need to achieve them first.
Bombing simply means chaining key-abilities together in order to burst down a group of enemies before giving them a chance to recover.

### Fun Coin

This is a resource earned throughout a GvG.
Commanders can use these to buy/activate buffs or debuffs for their team.

### Crowd Control

Going forward I will refer to this as “CC” and it refers to hindering, restricting or denying the enemies ability to move or take actions.

### Tenacity

Is a stat/condition that makes it harder for enemies to stagger you.

### Super Armor

Is a stat or condition that makes it impossible to stagger or CC you by any means other than executing or killing you.

### DoT

Short for “Damage over time” it refers to an effect, which deals continuous damage to an enemy.

Lastly, let us discuss what happens if you or the enemy manages to take down the goose thereforee start the last phase of the match.

### Carrying the tree

As soon as the Goose dies, the tree becomes accessible.

Now you need to act fast!

Here is what each member should do:

- One tank applies their shield and picks up the tree, moving towards their own base.

- The commanders should immediately use the “Express” Buff to make said tank move faster for 10 seconds.

- If the tank gets blocked or once you reach the first choke point (The gate you wish to exit through), the commander should use “Go Forward Indomitably”, which will make them immune to unit- and skill collision for 5 seconds.
  (Fox tip: let the tank walk a few steps after picking up, then trigger both buffs together to make the most of those 5 seconds.)

- At least one healer should stick with the tree carrier, to keep them alive

- Everyone else shifts their focus to defending the tree carrier and clearing out choke points

- IF you have Inkwell fans, you can use their EX-Skill to create areas/channels for your tree carrier, since they won’t get blocked by the walls, but it will be harder for enemies to reach them

- Skills like lion’s roar are exceptional for pushing enemies away from the carrier.

- If your team decides to attempt a bomb and lots of enemies grouped around the tree carrier, it can be beneficial for them to drop the tree for a few seconds to open up with their Mo-Blade EX-Skill and then pick up again once their team combos off of it.

Repeat these until you reach your home base, be especially mindful about the choke points in the middle of the map.

### Slowing down the tree

So, your Goose has fallen, and the enemy is beginning to move the tree, what now?

Stay calm when your Goose falls. The match is still open: your next objective is to prevent the enemy from leaving with the tree.

Where to focus your damage:

- It’s tempting to rush at the tree carrier, but darling, unless you have a way to shut down their healing (twinblade or gauntlet EX-Skill or Anti-heal debuff ready), you usually won’t be able to kill them just like that.
  Instead take out the healers first, again. Nothing changes here.

- Once the healers are down or antiheal is applied, focus the tree carrier.

How to slow down the tree:

- The most effective measure is to kill the person carrying the tree, but as mentioned, this is easier said than done

- The second-best option is stuns, you can still stun the tree carrier with Vernal umbrella shots and similar effects

- After this there are wind walls from either Inkwell fan martial art or even better, Inkwell Fan EX-Skill, they can hinder the carrier for long times and every second counts

- If none of these are available, body block him, this works especially well while using lions roar or dragon’s breath

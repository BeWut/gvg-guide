Win by breaking enemy bulwarks, defeating their Goose, and carrying their tree toward your own base. These pages explain the mechanics, roles, and coordination needed along the way.

This guide was adapted from **A Foxes Guide to GvG**. The original author draws on personal GvG experience reaching Jesting Sage in multiple guilds, online guides, and gameplay from global and CN guilds.

**Patch note:** The source does not specify a game patch. Timings, rewards, damage reduction, and skill effects below reflect the original guide and may change. Check numerical values against your current patch.

## Before your first match

- Unlock your weapon’s GvG EX skill in the guild Techniques menu.
- Prioritize shared GvG stat upgrades with your guild.
- Enable target distance; healers should review **Lowest HP** targeting.
- Know your squad, its color marker, and your attack or defense assignment.
- Follow the shot caller and coordinate engages rather than spending key cooldowns alone.

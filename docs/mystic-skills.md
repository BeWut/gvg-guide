## Generally Good

### Cloud Steps

![Mystic skills — source illustration 25](/images/gvg/image26.png)

Is mainly used as a gap closing tool to either escape or close distance on a target.

When near enemy towers it’s an easy way out by jumping onto the tower and then to a nearby roof.

### Lions Roar

![Mystic skills — source illustration 26](/images/gvg/image27.png)

Awesome skill in this mode.

The AoE silence is amazing, you can block off enemy tree carriers with it and it staggers where many other attacks don’t, due to the stagger reduction in GvG.

### Guardian Palm

![Mystic skills — source illustration 27](/images/gvg/image28.png)

The Ground version can be good for skirmishes and 1v1s.

The airborne version is a coinflip, if the enemy team doesn’t realize what you are doing it can turn around a fight but if they do, you will get focused down and die as soon as you hit the floor.

### Flaming Meteor

![Mystic skills — source illustration 28](/images/gvg/image29.png)

This might just be the best GvG Mystic skill.

A Group of people using this is like a bomb on its own.

It has great AoE Damage, AoE CC, lets you reposition, grants you damage reduction and super armor to get out of sticky situations.

### Soaring Spin

![Mystic skills — source illustration 29](/images/gvg/image30.png)

Lets you dodge some heavy hits, deals great single target damage and reduces healing.

This one is mainly good for skirmishes.

### Serene Breeze

![Mystic skills — source illustration 30](/images/gvg/image31.png)

It does what it always does: it breaks you out of any form of CC.

### Ghostly Steps

![Mystic skills — source illustration 31](/images/gvg/image32.png)

As a general player you want this to get out avoid damage with the improved dodges and to get around the map faster when the game assumes you are still in combat and can’t use your mount.

As a healer this is incredibly valuable to shake off those annoying twinbladers, following you around like your own paparazzi’s.

As a nameless you want this for the stamina cost reduction when using your charged heavies.

### Flute of the Tides

![Mystic skills — source illustration 32](/images/gvg/image33.png)

This skill is great for sieging towers, to bolster your ranged DPS capacities and if you try to wipe the enemy team as multiple flutes together can deal lots of damage to groups of enemies.

## Niche

### Leaping Toad

![Mystic skills — source illustration 33](/images/gvg/image34.png)

Provides good AoE damage, helps escape staggers.

If you don’t have access/vitality to/for better AoE skills, you can use this for bombing.

### Dragons Breath

![Mystic skills — source illustration 34](/images/gvg/image35.png)

Sadly, it doesn’t shine as much in this mode.

It grants some damage reduction and applies dot on the target.

The only real use for this is for a healer to hit a boss or the goose with it, to amplify their dps player’s damage.

### Drunken Poet

![Mystic skills — source illustration 35](/images/gvg/image36.png)

Poet can be used for small skirmishes or if you as a healer are really desperate for some damage but pretty much every AoE skill is a better option.

### 

### Yaksha Rush/Talon Strike

![Mystic skills — source illustration 36](/images/gvg/image37.png)

![Mystic skills — source illustration 37](/images/gvg/image38.png)

Outside of the halftime performance, these are really only used to gap close onto an enemy healer.

Blocks are very rare in GvG and therefore skills designed to break them simply aren’t as useful.

### Dragons Head

![Mystic skills — source illustration 38](/images/gvg/image39.png)

Dragons Head gained a new variant which is actually quite good for breaking bulwarks early on, outside of that it doesn’t find much use.

### Golden Body

![Mystic skills — source illustration 39](/images/gvg/image40.png)

On healers this can be run if you predict an incoming enemy bomb or if your reaction times are fast enough to prevent your team from getting wiped.

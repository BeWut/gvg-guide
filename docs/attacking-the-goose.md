Okay, so you took down 2 enemy Bulwarks and now gained access to their base.

Great, its time for the juicy part, the Goose!
Positioning is simple but there will be an important extra note once we went over each group

![Attacking the Goose — source illustration 57](/images/gvg/image58.png)

Attacking team ranged DPS:

As ranged DPS try to stand off to the sides and either follow up on your teammate’s bombs or focus down the Goose.

The latter is the most important part.

Try sticking close to those walls, near the entrances, you will see why in a bit.

Attacking team Tank and melee DPS:

While your main objective is of course taking down that bouncing feathered menace, you carry the responsibility of disrupting the enemy.

Group them up, kill them, do not let them play their game.

![Attacking the Goose — source illustration 58](/images/gvg/image59.png)

![Attacking the Goose — source illustration 59](/images/gvg/image60.png)

Attacking team Healer:

While it may be tempting to throw yourself in there, please play with caution.

If you die here, your team WILL get wiped and it’s a long way back from your base.

Jump in, drop your healing fields and get out again as fast as you can.

Support your team using flute and meteor mystic skills.

STAY ALIVE!

General Advice:

When trying to breach into the enemy’s base, don’t always take the gates.

Instead, jump over these walls, it’s easy, fast and most importantly: they can’t see you through them.

Your chances of being hit by a barrage of AoE damage are way smaller here.

In addition, both ranged DPS and healers should stick close to these, since they are an easy escape.

Dropped low? Just jump over the wall, heal up and get back in.

Don’t make your life harder by squeezing yourself through those choke points.

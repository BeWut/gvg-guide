## Teams

A small piece of advice, especially for beginner guilds in GvG is to mark players of the same team, in the same color (for example, marking team 1 in purple, team 2 with red, etc).

### Player Distribution

The most common approach is to split the guild into 6 teams.
3 for the attack and 3 for the defense with 5 people each

Should your guild have less active GvG players try to still maintain an even split between attack and defense teams and do not go lower then 4 players per team if possible.

### Team Composition

Due to how common Hybrid-builds are in GvG, I will not call out roles but weapons instead.

On the attacking team aim for:

- 1 Moblade

- 1 Panacea Fan

- 2 Ranged DPS (most commonly Nameless)

- 1 flex slot (usually dps)

On the defence team aim for:

- 1 Moblade

- 1 Panacea Fan

- 1 or more inkwell Fans

- 2 flex slots (usually dps)
